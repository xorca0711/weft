## Problem and intended outcome

The current coordinator ends at B3 source-bound assessments and proposed data/RQ
uses. Reading preferences and accurate summaries are useful preparation but do
not demonstrate that Weft can generate useful scientific evidence. The local
executor accepts synthetic fixtures only. The documented scientific route also
requires a new Ravel contract before a fresh analysis, coupling exploratory work
to the canonical research repository too early for the requested Weft-only work.

The target is a bounded loop: relevant paper → answerable question → qualified
data → reproducible analysis → numerical and scientific critique → revised
interpretation or next comparison. The result may support, challenge or fail to
resolve a proposed connection. No positive result or new RQ is required for a
successful, honestly reported run. A decision brief indexes the actual figures,
methods, QC and uncertainty; it is not the terminal deliverable.

## Proposed ownership boundary

Weft would manage provisional article-local workspaces with versioned plans,
contracts, analysis adapters, input references and receipts. These are candidate
evidence, not a second canonical question register. Ravel would continue to own
accepted research questions, methods, evidence and acceptance history. Pinned
Ravel context remains read-only until an explicitly authorized integration.

This is a proposed change to the current Ravel-first execution ownership in
[RAVEL_CONNECTION](RAVEL_CONNECTION.md). **W01 must review and resolve that
boundary before enabling the new route.** Reuse the scientific contract/runner
where compatible; qualify any adapter and output-root separation explicitly.
Do not copy a validator and remove checks, redirect a historical script in place,
or treat this document as an exception to current instructions or policy flags.
Existing Choi contracts and outputs stay in their current locations.

The proposed workspace has immutable source/input manifests, a biological-unit
and shared-data map, purpose and comparison, endpoint, exposure/amendment record,
code/environment identity, operational limits, attempt receipts, numerical
outputs, critiques and owner dispositions. Exact path/schema choices belong to
W01. Large data and private source texts stay outside public Git. An optional RQ
link is a pinned reference, never a fabricated canonical ID.

## Scientific and operational state

The new scientific case would progress through proposed → data-qualified →
contract-frozen → executed → results-verified → scientifically-reviewed →
disposition-recorded → export-proposed → integrated. This is a proposed state
model, not a list of currently available commands.

Operational attempt state is separate: a process can fail, be cancelled or have
an unknown outcome at any scientific stage. Unknown outcomes retain their lease
and block duplicate execution; a hold does not become a retry. An unsuccessful
reproduction can be a valid scientific result. Execution success cannot promote
scientific acceptance. Changed inputs or comparisons create new versions, retain
prior outcomes and invalidate affected unstarted work explicitly.

Reading-only, infeasible, deferred and no-further-analysis outcomes remain useful
terminal dispositions. An extension is optional and needs its own bounded
rationale; the pipeline does not automatically search for a favorable result.

## Phase 4 and the scientist's role

Phase 4 should develop and evaluate evidence generation alongside source/retrieval
quality. Keep the existing frozen 12-paper package and its unpassed status. Its
source audits are reusable preparation, not a substitute for numerical evidence
or independent reference judgments. Any changed evaluation scope/criteria receive
a prospective amendment with prior exposure retained; do not retroactively score
the old reading benchmark as passed or require every paper to yield an analysis.

Before execution, the scientist resolves consequential choices about the question,
comparison, intended inference and alternatives. AI prepares concrete options and
the analysis plan; routine technical choices stay within the approved contract.
After execution, the scientist works from actual plots, discrepancies and critiques
to refine interpretation and the next informative comparison. Standing approved
scope can cover routine work; material design changes are explicit amendments.

One case can establish workflow feasibility. General scientific-quality claims
need a separate prospective evaluation of source fidelity, analysis correctness,
unit handling, robustness and interpretation against suitable independent
references. Same-data reanalysis is not independent replication. Cell-state scores
or ligand/receptor expression alone do not establish fate, secretion or causality.
